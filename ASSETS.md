Textures are fetched by `tools/scripts/fetch_textures.py` (fortress) and
`tools/scripts/fetch_character_textures.py` (characters).

Characters: gear, uniforms, the carbine blockout and the hand-keyed clips
(aim/fire, reload, prone, lean) are made by our own scripts
(`tools/blender/characters/`). The locomotion clips (idle, walk, run,
crouch) are CMU motion capture retargeted onto each operator at build time;
see "Motion capture" below and DESIGN.md, "Realism pass: character
animation".

Sounds: weapons, footsteps, bullet impacts, breaking surfaces and
explosions are recorded sounds from CC0 and CC-BY packs, listed under
"Audio" below (CC-BY credits are in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)). The round and
gadget sounds (defuser beeps, result chords, dart hiss, stake tick:
`src/round/sound.rs`) are still synthesised in code.

Models made in code (no external assets): the defuser device
(`src/round/defuser.rs`), sensor darts and seismic stakes (`src/gadgets/`),
and the bomb-site floor markings (`src/round/sites.rs`).

Weapons: the models in your hands and on characters are the CC0 AKM and
Service Pistol above (`tamarisk.glb`, `acacia.glb`). The scripted blockouts
are kept as fallbacks, made in this repo with no external source:
`assets/models/weapons/tamarisk_blockout.glb` and `acacia_blockout.glb`,
generated by `tools/blender/weapons/build_weapons.py` (untextured; their
materials are defined in `src/weapons/materials.rs`). The muzzle-flash
texture is generated in code (`src/weapons/viewmodel.rs`). The source
models are not in the repo; `tools/scripts/import_weapons.sh` rebuilds the
game files from the staged downloads.

Destruction (M4) is procedural (cell-grid meshes, debris, dust, the breach
charge and reinforcement plates) and reuses the library materials above; it
adds no external assets.

Gadgets, drones and CCTV (M6 part two) are made in code too, with no
external assets: the ring cutter, backplate charges, grenades, razor wire,
ballistic cover, proximity alarm, recon drone and CCTV models (primitive
shapes), the smoke / dust / spark puff textures and the drone and camera
overlay (grain, scanlines, vignette) generated as images, and every gadget
sound synthesised in `src/gadgets/sfx.rs`.

## Audio

Everything under `assets/audio/` is built from these packs by
`tools/audio/build_audio.py` (fixed slice times and layer choices; see the
README). Only CC0 and CC-BY packs are used; the Sonniss GDC bundles are not
(their licence forbids redistributing the files). CC-BY attribution lines
are copied exactly into [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

Every shipped file was: decoded, resampled to 48 kHz, DC-offset removed,
high-passed (35-120 Hz) to drop rumble, trimmed with a short lead-in and a
faded tail, loudness-matched within its bank (A-weighted) and peak-normalised
(-1 to -3 dBFS), and encoded as Ogg Vorbis (112 kbps mono; 160 kbps stereo).
Mono except the first-person shots (stereo). Extra changes per pack below.
